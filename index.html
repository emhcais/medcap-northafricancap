Guide

How to use this map

  1. Search both names. Original and baptismal names have equal weight in search and display.
  2. Change the location view. “No location data” keeps all 92 people without a recorded origin accessible.
  3. Filter by year or Papal Period. The controls combine, and Reset Filters restores the full collection.
  4. Select a map point or record. Map points aggregate people at the same reviewed origin; every record also remains available in the list.
  5. Read the evidence notes. A record identifies inherited dates, inferred papal periods, generalized regions, and unresolved places.

Interpretive caution

The map visualizes documented origins, not travel routes, destinations, or baptism locations. Rome is intentionally not plotted. Absence of a map point does not remove a person from the collection.
